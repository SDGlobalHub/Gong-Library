Indeed weaknesses to probe

✓ Do

  • Acknowledge Indeed's strengths (active seekers, high-volume roles)
  • Position LinkedIn as complementary first, then a replacement
  • Use questions to surface Indeed pain — never criticize directly
  • Focus on outcomes: time-to-hire, cost-per-qualified-candidate, quality
  • Create curiosity about LinkedIn's unique capabilities (passive, targeting)
  • Transition to AE meeting with a specific value (comparison, ROI, candidate preview)

✕ Don't

  • Bash Indeed — sounds defensive and unprofessional
  • Discuss pricing details — that's the AE's role
  • Offer demos yourself — position the AE meeting as the next step
  • Assume Indeed isn't working — discover their actual experience first
  • Get into feature wars — focus on business outcomes and pain
  • Let "we use Indeed" stop the conversation — most clients use multiple channels

🎯 Remember

Your goal is to book the meeting, not close the deal. Create enough curiosity and uncover enough pain that the prospect wants to learn more. The AE handles pricing, demos, and closing.
